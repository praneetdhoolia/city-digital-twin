# Sampling and comparability families — current position

*A position page states the CURRENT truth for one topic. It is rewritten at every `/handoff` that touches the topic; the dated history and every rationale live in [`DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) at the sections cited. Nothing here is a result: no run since family F4 has passed its gate.*

**Updated:** 1 September 2026 · **Record read through:** §9.136 · **Open family:** F22

## What is built

- **The sampling unit is the household.** `RUN.sample.unit` = `household` (`derived`). The sampler keeps a household when blake2b(`household|<id>|RUN.machine.seed`) / 2^64 falls below the fraction, so whole households are kept, the sample nests (1% is a strict subset of 10%), and every household-coupled mechanism is fraction-independent by construction (§9.45). The external and through tiers carry no household and hash on their own id (§9.45). `RUN.machine.seed` is 20260810, the same value as `B.seed.master`.
- **Lift couplings extend the unit.** The sampler union-finds (`householdId`, `liftHousehold`) pairs, so a passenger and the household that drives them are kept or dropped together (§9.60, §9.127). Shared-ride driver households are named separately in `sharedDriverHousehold` and excluded from those unions, so the clusters stay the small lift couplings (§9.127).
- **A shared pair shares a sampling-hash bucket.** `B.ride.shared_lift_hash_bucket` 0.05 (`assumed`, sweep [0.05, 0.1]): the fourth binder pass (`B.ride.shared_lift_scope` = `same_sa2_od`) binds a passenger only to drivers whose household hash sits in the same bucket of that width, so every nested sample at a fraction that is a multiple of the width keeps both members, and a bucket prefers no hash (§9.129). It replaced the §9.127 at-or-below rule. The binder records the seed it hashed under; a run under another `RUN.machine.seed` breaks the guarantee and the report names the seed that holds it (§9.127).
- **The sample count is asserted at rebuild.** A 10% draw must land within 8.5–11.5% of persons before any smoke or launch (§9.127).
- **Run identity is the full key.** `find_completed` matches scenario, day, fraction, iterations, seed, `--set` overrides, the warm-start key, `controler_sha256`, `values_sha256` over every resolved registry value (§9.104) and `inputs_sha256`, the day's population file hash (§9.127), written to `_meta.json` and `_run.json` and declared in `config/schema/outputs/meta.schema.json` and `config/schema/outputs/run.schema.json`. A record without a hash never matches, by design (§9.104). Never compare across fractions, across families, or across a network build (§3.5, §9.10, §9.12).
- **Run directories are named by the runner**, never by hand: `<launch yyyymmddThhmmss>_<iterations>it_<sample pct>pct`. A dead run is renamed `aborted_<name>` in place and its `_meta.json` carries `status` and `cause`; `_run.json`, written only on success, remains the result gate (§9.65, §9.66).
- **The run index** `results/INDEX.md` and `results/INDEX.csv` are regenerated by `src/analyse/build_run_index.py` (#77): every run directory carries its family, read from `cities/newcastle/docs/run_families.json` and never re-derived, and its validity (`aborted` / `failed`; `probe` under 250 iterations; `arm`, with relaxation as its own column), so a cross-family or cross-fraction comparison cannot pass unnoticed.

## The families

A family boundary is a recorded model, data or network change after which nothing compares to what ran before it (§3.5). A run belongs to the newest family whose `from_launch` stamp is at or below its launch stamp; the `overrides` block of `cities/newcastle/docs/run_families.json` wins where the record attributes a run explicitly. A new family is declared in that file in the same change as its `DECISIONS.md` entry.

## What is measured

- Person sampling dissolved households: ride trips in a household that drives at all were 32.6% on `conv1000_10pct` and 43.1% on `conv1000_25pct`, the fraction deciding the mechanism (§9.45).
- 1% is unusable: flow-capacity granularity left 1,032 car legs aborted at the 30 h horizon at 1% against 4 at 10%, and 380 PT passengers boarded and never alighted (§9.12). From 10% to 25% the fraction sensitivity flattened, car +1.6 pp and ride −1.1 pp (§9.12).
- Unioning shared-ride households made the sampling unit a giant component: the first F18 arm `20260830T161243_300it_10pct` held 31,262 persons where 62,134 were due; a directed closure landed at 17.65% of persons (§9.127).
- The at-or-below rule kept named drivers at 12.4% and everyone else at 7.95% of a 10% draw; the eligible non-named pool at 6.1%, the motorbike carve at 5.5%, the truck carve at 5.1% (§9.129).
- Bucket width costs candidate supply, not bound trips (WEEKDAY servable / bound / short): at-or-below 98,549 / 59,718 / 0; bucket 0.10 86,848 / 59,806 / 0; bucket 0.05 73,509 / 59,701 / 0; unconstrained 105,515 / 59,648 / 17 (§9.129).
- The last asserted sample: 62,134 of 620,553 persons, 10.01% (§9.127). The population has since been rebuilt at 612,634 persons and the chains, plans and run inputs have not (§9.131), so that count is stale.
- Resume matched the wrong run twice: the test overlay was handed the control `20260829T205738_40it_1pct` (§9.104), and a rebuild smoke passed by resuming the earlier probe on the old plans (§9.127).
- pt2matsim: stop-to-link assignment agrees 100.000% between builds, route link sequences 81.9–82.3% (§3.5); one build per comparison.

## What is open

- The first F22 arm runs at 25% × 300 (user decision at approval, 31 Aug): the §9.129 bucket rule keeps shared pairs at any fraction that is a multiple of 0.05, so 25% keeps them; the cost of the choice is that its readings never compare with the 10% F21 gate (§9.10, §9.12), only with its own targets.
- The confirmation arm after the loop: whether a separate 25% confirmation is still needed now that the loop itself runs at 25% (§9.129) is the user's call at convergence.
- The design-effect penalty of household cluster sampling is unestimated and no seed-variance measurement exists; `n_replications` stays 30 (§9.45). The threshold between 10% and 25% is unmeasured (§9.12).
- One arm at a time; the machine-level stall that hit two concurrent arms is #66.
- `aborted_20260818T162538_1000it_25pct` stays unattributed to a family.

## Refused — do not re-raise

- Comparing across a sample fraction (§9.10, §9.12), across a family, or across a network build or schedule mapping (§3.5).
- Sampling by person, and a side file for household membership (§9.45).
- A directed closure over driver households: nested, but 17.65% of persons and weighted to car-owning households (§9.127).
- The at-or-below coupling rule: the count was right, the composition was not (§9.129).
- Reading a 1% smoke's pairing: 1% is not a multiple of the bucket width and breaks pairs (§9.129).
- Hand-named run directories and the `--tag` flag (§9.65); a harness that deletes a stale result (§9.65).
- Resuming a record without `values_sha256` or `inputs_sha256` (§9.104, §9.127).
- Deriving a family inside the index from the launch date alone: the JSON is the record (`src/analyse/build_run_index.py`).
