## What is built

- **Both plan-removal paths honour `RUN.replanning.plan_selector_for_removal`** (§9.166, #174). `EscortCoherenceListener.trim()` removed the lowest-scored plan by its own rule whenever a coherence proposal overfilled plan memory, so the `SelectRandom` control would have switched MATSim's removal and not the listener's; it now injects the selector MATSim binds and re-selects at random if the selector took the selected plan, as `GenericStrategyManagerImpl.removePlans` does (`tests/unit/test_plan_removal_one_selector.py`). `GatedSubtourModeChoice` treats a plan whose subtour decomposition throws as MIXED, not clean. The controler was recompiled; F33 has no reading, and the first arm past that boundary is F35's arm 0 `20260912T202242_300it_25pct` (§9.169).

- **A seeded plan carries PER-TRIP modes, not one mode per tour** (§9.143). That is what lets a partially bound tour ride its covered leg while the uncovered one takes `B.mode.partial_bind_base` = `pt`, keeping the whole subtour non-chain so the chain/non-chain mix `ChooseRandomLegModeForSubtour` refuses (§9.119) is unreachable by construction. Plan memory now peaks at **7 of `RUN.replanning.max_agent_plan_memory`** = 8; `check_package.py` reads that cap from the registry rather than a hard-coded count, because MATSim removes an UNSCORED plan first when memory overflows and a seed wider than the memory silently loses part of the choice set.
- **The seed is the choice set.** `B.mode.seed_method` = `full_choice_set` (§9.120). `src/build/build_matsim_plans.py` writes one plan per mode the person may use — car where a car is available, walk, bike where one is available and the person is old enough, pt, taxi where old enough — each mode on every tour it may take, serving tours held at car, and one further plan riding the covered tours where the demand named a driver. No mode is favoured: each is one plan, once. WEEKDAY carries 2–6 plans per person and 9,880,427 seeded legs against 2,343,321 in the selected plans (§9.126).
- **`uniform_draw` is retained as the sweep alternative** — the pre-§9.120 single plan, tour modes drawn from `B.mode.seed_split` (uniform over the usable modes, deliberately a bad guess) — so how much the search decided is a measurement, not a claim. `B.mode.seed_split_informed` (approximately the observed split) also survives as a declared alternative, never the default.
- **The first-executed plan is drawn uniformly** over the person's seeded plans by a sha256 hash of the person id and the master seed (`seedorder|<pid>|20260810` in `src/build/build_matsim_plans.py`), carried as the `selected="yes"` flag, so iteration 0 is a mixed traffic state like every later one (§9.121). No rng stream, no new value, no re-scoring or warm-up.
- **Plan memory** `RUN.replanning.max_agent_plan_memory` = 8, raised from 5 inside its 3–10 sweep (§9.120): MATSim executes an unscored plan before it consults the selector, and `WorstPlanForRemovalSelector` drops an unscored plan first on overflow, so memory must exceed the seeded plan count or seeded modes are discarded before they are ever executed.
- **Mode choice** is MATSim's `SubtourModeChoice` wrapped by `citysim.GatedSubtourModeChoice` (`src/java/citysim/GatedSubtourModeChoice.java`). Declared: `RUN.mode_choice.modes` car, ride, pt, bike, walk, taxi; `RUN.mode_choice.chain_based_modes` car, bike; `RUN.mode_choice.consider_car_availability` true; `RUN.mode_choice.subtour_behavior` betweenAllAndFewerConstraints; `RUN.mode_choice.proba_random_single_trip_mode` 0.5 (§9.92); `RUN.mode_choice.coord_distance_m` 100 (§9.119).
- **Strategy mix** `RUN.replanning.weights`: ChangeExpBeta 0.7, ReRoute 0.15, SubtourModeChoice 0.1, TimeAllocationMutator 0.05 (`RUN.replanning.time_mutation_range_s` 1800; `RUN.replanning.strategy_subpopulations` limits SubtourModeChoice to `person`). Selection is ChangeExpBeta on score: the gate and the listeners propose, never impose (§9.92).
- **A leaf subtour mix is repaired at the seed** (§9.140, #96): `leaf_mixed_tours()` in `src/build/build_matsim_plans.py` reproduces `TripStructureUtils.getSubtours(plan, coordDistance)` — the latest unallocated trip whose origin lies within `RUN.mode_choice.coord_distance_m` of the current destination closes the loop, a contiguous loop is a leaf — and drives the offending free tour of a car-available person's variant instead of offering a plan MATSim cannot hold. Rebuilt plans: WEEKDAY 3 tours on 1 person, SAT 9 on 2, SUN 0 (`_plans_report.json`, `leaf_mix_repairs`).
- **The gate refuses a proposal whole** and restores the pre-innovation plan; it never replaces a draw and never edits a memory. Four refusals: a trip beyond the declared reach (bounds 0.0 since §9.106, so inert); a proposal that would leave a subtour mixing chain- and non-chain modes (§9.119); `ride` on a trip outside the person's `boundRideTrips`, or a declared driver taken off `car` on a trip in `boundDriveTrips` — per-trip attributes written from the binding tables (§9.120); and a plan that ARRIVES mixed is stood aside from mode choice altogether while `ReRoute` still runs — a refusal to crash on #96, not its repair.
- **Escort and joint coherence listeners** offer a decohered bound pair its coherent plan back at `B.ride.escort_coherence_rate` / `B.ride.joint_coherence_rate` = 0.4 (§9.93); the conversion walks to the ROOT subtour so no enclosing subtour is left mixed (§9.118).
- **Innovation cutoff** `RUN.replanning.fraction_to_disable_innovation` = 0.8, sweep 0.7–0.9 (§9.7). At the cutoff selection concentrates every agent onto its best remembered plan in ONE iteration — a property of the structure, not drift (§9.43).
- **One listener had been innovating past that cutoff, and every relaxation verdict rests on it** (§9.158). `citysim.EscortCoherenceListener` added AND selected plans on every iteration with no cutoff of its own, so plans were still being CREATED through the innovation-off tail — and `summarise_run.relaxation` declares a run relaxed on the stated basis that none are. It now reads the cutoff from `replanning().getFractionOfIterationsToDisableInnovation()` (API verified with `javap` against the pinned `matsim-2027.0-2026w25.jar`) and past it MEASURES decoherence and proposes nothing. No recorded verdict is retracted; F35's arm 0 `20260912T202242_300it_25pct` ran past its cutoff on this listener, so its relaxation verdict rests on a tail that is genuinely innovation-free (§9.169). **The controler is recompiled and green** (§9.158), so the deployed bytecode changed and the next arm opened a comparability family.
- **Iteration count** `RUN.controler.last_iteration` = **250**, declared 14 September 2026 in place of 1000 (§9.169; sweep 250–2000, §9.43): innovation off at 200 (`RUN.replanning.fraction_to_disable_innovation` 0.8), a 50-iteration tail, and the 30 run-input sets re-assembled so the shipped configs carry it — only `lastIteration` changed; the S2 × WEEKDAY config diff is that one line. Evidence: arm 0's post-settle drift of 0.128 pp max and an innovated state that moved at most +0.42 pp (car, planned 64.71 → 65.13 %) between it.200 and it.240; it saves 3.8–4.6 h per 25 % arm. `run_matsim.py --iterations` has no default; arm 0 and every gate arm since F15 ran 300 with innovation off at 240 under their run overlays (§9.120).
- **Relaxation instrument**: `RUN.relaxation.settle_margin_iterations` = 10 (sweep 1–100, §9.43) opens the drift window after the one-iteration snap; `RUN.relaxation.drift_tolerance_pp` = 0.5 (sweep 0.1–1.0). `src/analyse/summarise_run.py` reports `snap_pp`, `drift_pp` (the verdict) and `cutoff_to_final_pp` (the old instrument, kept auditable).
- **The gate is read on the trend, not the level**, against a scored choice set: iterations 0–6 execute the unscored seeds, 10–30 are exploration (§9.108, §9.120; brief directive 3). The reader is `src/analyse/report_mode_ridership.py` over `src/analyse/iteration_trips.py`, validated against `<n>.trips.csv.gz` wherever both exist (§9.120).
- **Choice-set coverage is an instrument, and the gate now carries its bound** (§9.163). `src/analyse/report_choice_set_coverage.py` reads MATSim's own `modeChoiceCoverage1x.txt`, which every arm has written since the beginning and nothing in this repository read. A scoring constant reallocates between plans an agent already holds, so a mode's coverage is an arithmetic ceiling on its share and `coverage − share` is the most any constant for it can add. `report_mode_ridership.py` prints that bound beside every gate verdict; it never changes the verdict, which stays the goal's. Two refusals are built in: a **locked carve** (motorbike, truck — the person holds no alternative, so share may exceed coverage) is reported with no bound, and a target on another **denominator** (heavy and light rail are boardings per weekday) is not compared with a percentage at all.
- **Score averaging is declared and shipped OFF** (§9.163). `RUN.replanning.score_msa_representation` = `absent` (categorical, `absent` | `at_innovation_cutoff`, `sweep_role: answer`) with the computed companion `RUN.replanning.score_msa_fraction` bound to `scoring.fractionOfIterationsToStartScoreMSA` and `derived_from` the gate and the innovation cutoff. At `absent` it emits the literal MATSim's own parameter dump records for its default, so declaring it was byte-neutral; at `at_innovation_cutoff` it emits `RUN.replanning.fraction_to_disable_innovation` and introduces **no new number**. Until now every plan in every arm carried the score of its LAST execution, so at the cutoff every agent selected the maximum of noisy single-execution scores at once.

- **The choice-set branch has a control for the first time** (§9.164, #174, #155). `RUN.replanning.plan_selector_for_removal` is declared - `WorstPlanSelector` shipped, sweep over MATSim's own five (`SelectRandom`, `SelectExpBetaForRemoval`, `ChangeExpBetaForRemoval`, `PathSizeLogitSelectorForRemoval`), `sweep_role: answer` - with `RUN.scoring.path_size_logit_beta` declared beside it, because `PathSizeLogitSelectorForRemoval` is one of its members and an arm that spent it would otherwise spend that undeclared value too. It ships at the framework's own value, so declaring it changed nothing. `SelectRandom` is the control arm that breaks the scoring-to-membership feedback.
- **The declared passenger is put on `ride` at the seed** (§9.164, #86, #48). `B.mode.bound_passenger_placement` = `every_plan` (sweep `alternative`, `sweep_role: answer`) puts a round-trip-covered tour on `ride` in EVERY seeded plan, which is what the demand already did for the other half of the same pair - a serving tour reads `car` in every plan. A partially bound tour keeps the per-trip override on `B.mode.partial_bind_base`, so the subtour stays non-chain and §9.119's refused state is still unreachable by construction. Duplicate plans created when two bases collapse onto one assignment are FOLDED, not seeded - and where folding would leave a person holding ONE plan, an alternative on their first base mode is kept, because a seed that leaves an agent no alternative in memory is the failure §9.120 measured (65 % of the agents still cycling at the F14 gate held no bike-free plan at all).
- **A gate-stopped arm is readable by the fit pipeline** (§9.158): `extract_metrics` falls back to `ITERS/it.<reached>/` when `completion` is not `ran_to_last_iteration`.

## What is open

- **Why convergence makes the fit worse is now the open question of the project** (§9.162). The post-cutoff level is car-heavier than anything the gates read, and the snap says the agents' best plans were always more car-heavy than their sampled behaviour. Whether the cause sits in the scoring function, in the choice set (pt reaches 25.78 % of agents on F32, 17.53 % on arm 0) or in the routers is not settled by either result; arm 0 measured it again — planned car 62.26 % at it.100 → 66.94 % at 300, snap +1.683 pp (§9.169) — and separating those is what the pairs must do (#172).
- **Five one-field controls, one control arm, and the order is still the operator's** (§9.164, §9.169). The five are: scoring (`RUN.replanning.score_msa_representation`), the choice set (`RUN.replanning.plan_selector_for_removal`), the routers (`C.raptor.mode_cost_representation`), the demand (`B.mode.bound_passenger_placement`, already SPENT - it ships at `every_plan`), and service quality (`C.time_weights.service_quality_representation`, #175). F35's arm 0 `20260912T202242_300it_25pct` is the CONTROL HALF of every pair (#172); the user chose no arm this session, so the order is undecided and no approval stands. The recommendation on arm 0's evidence is the **routers pair first**: three of the six STOP modes are pt submodes decided in the raptor (§9.169). A pair arm runs the declared 250 (cutoff 200) and differences against arm 0's it.300 reading; the post-cutoff drift of 0.128 pp is inside the tolerance either way.
- **Superseded** (§9.163 said two of three had a control and the third did not). The scoring branch: `RUN.replanning.score_msa_representation` = `at_innovation_cutoff`, which is the only candidate that predicts BOTH measured symptoms — the +2.211 pp car snap and the average plan score peaking at 19.2049 then falling to 14.5679. The router branch: `C.raptor.mode_cost_representation` = `mode_constant`, built and deployed and never run (§9.162, #49). The choice-set branch has a named mechanism (#174) and no control until `planSelectorForRemoval` is declared (#155). **Each is an arm and each opens a family**, so the order they are spent in is the decision #172 asks for and none may be launched before that design exists.
- **A crowding or raptor arm can move coverage as a side effect** (#174, #98, #49). Making a mode's plans score worse makes them the ones evicted, so a paired arm can report a share fall that is a smaller choice set rather than a changed preference. Read coverage on both arms of any pair, or the paired difference is not attributable.
- **Superseded: the horizon is declared 250** (§9.169, the user's decision). §9.142 reviewed re-declaring `RUN.controler.last_iteration` to 250 and REFUSED, because §9.7 had measured 250 insufficient — but that measurement was pre-rebuild, at 1 % and under the uniform seed. The field now reads 250 (was 1000) with the cutoff at 200 (fraction 0.8), on the evidence of a result: arm 0's post-settle drift of 0.128 pp max and an innovated state that moved ≤0.42 pp between it.200 and it.240. Requirement 8's 250 is declared on a measurement rather than typed against one; the shipped configs were re-assembled to carry it.
- **Whether 200 iterations of SEARCH suffice stays unmeasured** (§9.169): the pair arms cut innovation at 200 where arm 0 cut at 240, and the ≤0.42 pp movement between those points on arm 0 bounds the difference without testing it. The 1500-iteration arm was cancelled (§9.43, limit 1).
- **The `full_choice_set` against `uniform_draw` sweep** has not been run on one family; how much the search decided remains unmeasured (`B.mode.seed_method` sweep basis).
- **Each seeded plan is scored once** under the traffic of the iteration it ran in; only re-execution updates it, and how fast selection refines that is what a gate reads (§9.121).
- **Whether the choice-set decay above is a defect or MATSim's ordinary memory is the next arm's question** (§9.140): a mode whose plan trails the winner by a few utils (the ferry's median 2.8, [light-rail-and-ferry](light-rail-and-ferry.md)) is dropped and must be re-proposed — not a constant to move.
